Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your phone

This policy explains what Petify, an Android app developed by Ali Hesham, does with your information. The short version: we do not collect any of it.

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Petify has no server and does not request internet access. Nothing you enter in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

Information stored by the app

To work, Petify saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • The username and email address you register with, and your password in the form of a salted one-way hash. The password itself is never stored.
  • The pets you add, your adoption requests and your favourites.
  • Your settings: theme, language and whether you are signed in.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive or have access to your information.
  • We do not share or sell information to third parties.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app asks for no device permissions: no location, camera, contacts or files.

Backups and moving to a new phone

Petify's data is excluded from Android cloud backup, so it is not uploaded to your Google account.

If you set up a new phone by copying directly from your old one, Android may copy Petify's data between your two devices. That copy stays under your control and never reaches us.

Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Passwords are saved only as salted one-way hashes (PBKDF2), so they cannot be read back from the device.

Anyone who can unlock your phone can open Petify while you are signed in, so keep your device protected with a screen lock.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen inside the app, and nothing is retained afterwards.

Account & data deletion

Delete your Petify account and data

Petify, by Ali Hesham, keeps your account only on your own phone. You can delete it from inside the app in a few seconds. Here is how, and how to reach us if you need a hand.

Petify has no server and does not use the internet. We hold no copy of your account, pets or requests, so deleting them from your phone deletes them for good.

How to delete your account

Delete from inside the app Recommended

Removes your account, adoption requests, favourites and the pets you added. Keeps the app and your theme and language settings.

  1. Open Petify and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete Account and confirm.

Clear the app's data Keeps the app

Removes every account and all data, and leaves Petify installed.

  1. Open your phone's Settings › Apps › Petify.
  2. Tap Storage.
  3. Tap Clear data (on some phones: Clear storage) and confirm.

Uninstall Petify

Removes the app together with every account and all data.

  1. Press and hold the Petify icon.
  2. Choose Uninstall and confirm.

Sign Out on the Profile screen only signs you out. It does not delete anything.

Deleting from inside the app keeps your theme and language settings. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes those too.

Nothing else is kept afterwards and there is no retention period, because no data is stored outside your device. Deletion cannot be undone.

Request deletion or ask for help

Can't reach your phone, or the steps above did not work? Send us a request and we will reply by email within 7 days. Because your data exists only on your device, we cannot delete it remotely, but we will guide you through it.

The button opens your email app with the request ready to send. You can also write to us directly at alyheshamali@gmail.com
